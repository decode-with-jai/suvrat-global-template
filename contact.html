Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

What services do you offer?

+

SUVRAT GLOBAL offers three primary services: IT Services (web development, mobile apps, cloud solutions, cybersecurity), Road Construction (highway projects, urban roads, infrastructure development), and Print & Scanning Services (document printing, photo printing, scanning, and digitization).

How do I get a quote for my project?

+

You can request a quote by filling out our contact form on this page, calling us directly, or emailing us with details about your project. Our team will review your requirements and provide a customized quote within 48 hours.

What is your turnaround time?

+

Turnaround time varies depending on project scope and complexity. For printing services, we typically deliver within 2-5 business days. IT projects range from 4-16 weeks depending on requirements. Construction projects have customized timelines based on project size.

Do you provide ongoing support?

+

Yes! We provide comprehensive support and maintenance services for all our projects. This includes software updates, bug fixes, infrastructure maintenance, and technical support to ensure your solutions continue to perform optimally.

Can you handle large-scale projects?

+

Absolutely! Our team has extensive experience handling large-scale projects across all three service areas. We have successfully completed projects worth crores of rupees with multiple teams working simultaneously.

What are your payment terms?

+

We offer flexible payment terms tailored to your project needs. Typically, we require 30-50% advance payment to initiate the project, with the remainder due upon completion. For large projects, milestone-based payments are available.

Are you certified?

+

Yes! SUVRAT GLOBAL holds ISO 9001:2015 certification ensuring quality management standards across all operations. We also maintain industry-specific certifications and comply with all relevant regulations.

Do you work with international clients?

+

Yes, we have successfully served international clients and are experienced in handling cross-border projects. We can work with different time zones and currencies, and provide communication in multiple languages.
